Borrower paths

One shell, different borrower realities.

FHC is designed to sit across three common paths without pretending they are the same file.

PAYG

Employee borrower

Income consistency, liabilities, expenses, deposit position, and whether an old pre-approval still deserves confidence.

Business

Self-employed / SME

Income evidence, business trading history, add-backs, tax position, and how clearly the story can be explained.

Investor

Existing owner / investor

Equity, cash-flow pressure, existing debt structure, rental assumptions, and when another review is sensible.
